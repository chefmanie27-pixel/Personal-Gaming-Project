## Durable alert scheduling and history checkpoint
Created additive migration 20261103180000_durable_alerts with DurableAlertJob (singleton cursor/due time/lease/retry state) and AlertDelivery (private observation-level record). DURABLE_ALERTS_ENABLED defaults to 0; configured .env and existing database were not migrated or activated. Legacy mode stays compatible and does not query new tables. Migration applied only to fresh disposable databases.

Durable mode polls every 5 seconds, claims by a database-clock 90-second renewable UUID lease, fences every delivery transaction and completion, and checkpoints batches of at most 100. Partial batches are due after 1 second; completed passes wait 15 minutes. Failed batches preserve the checkpoint and persist 15/30/60/120/240-second capped retry delays with a fixed sanitized error code. Lease expiry allows a replacement worker to resume. Job-row initialization is separately committed to avoid shared-lock upgrade deadlocks; claim retries transient deadlocks up to three attempts. Notification, lastTriggered and unique delivery record commit together. Replays retain deduplication even if the timestamp is reset. Current alert target/currency/state and workspace version are checked before writing; opt-out creates an honest disabled outcome rather than sending anything.

Account history is authenticated, cursor-paginated and account-scoped; it shows real observed/recorded dates, matching rule and delivered/disabled/duplicate outcome. Deleting an alert preserves records; deleting an account cascades them. Old notifications are not reconstructed. Capability gating hides the panel when disabled. Browser checks cover history failure/retry/pagination and 320px layout.

All 91 backend tests pass after the concurrency fix; 22 frontend tests, full browser suite, typecheck/build, Prisma validation and diff hygiene pass.
